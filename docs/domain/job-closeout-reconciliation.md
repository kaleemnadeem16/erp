# Job closeout and historical reconciliation

## Where it sits

Completed/eligible terminated Job → owning-source reconciliation → atomic closeout evidence.
Later evidence → classified owning correction/resolution → coverage reassessment/current restatement.
Sales customer obligations, supplier commitments, stock and Finance retain independent ownership.

## Evidence and applicability — no physical schema

Each owning readiness result retains company/Job/source family/component, applicability basis/source
inventory, scope/generation, actor/service/time, facts checked, remaining effects and owning receipt.
Outcomes: Resolved; Supported valuation follow-up; Blocking obligation/discrepancy; Unsupported/unknown;
Not applicable with evidence. These are readiness outcomes, not lifecycle/valuation/coverage states.
Unavailable adapter cannot report N/A/NONE. SIMPLE shows only actionable outstanding issues; no
universal manual checklist, independent approval or optional timesheet/cost/declaration requirement.

Closeout evidence pins eligible lifecycle/holds/termination, completion/remainder, source-generation
vector/readiness results, disposition receipts, follow-ups, applicable approval/current authority and
exact-intent operation receipt. Original closeout stays immutable; current discrepancy is separate.

## Supported valuation follow-up

Only source-confirmed resolved performance, scope and operational responsibility permit this route.
Retain source/component; unresolved valuation fact; reason/basis including known versus unknown amount;
eligible accountable owner; real supported owning correction operation; acknowledgement; review date
or event trigger; status/history and successor links. Assignment/free text alone never suffices.
Governed reassignment requires eligible successor acknowledgement/history before relinquishing old
responsibility; absence/deactivation must not orphan a follow-up. Dates trigger accountability, not waiver.

Unresolved performance quantities/rejection, hire periods, custody, ownership, allocations or unknown
downstream effects block. No general dispute override. Monetary difference qualifies only after owning
confirmation and supported later reconciliation; otherwise block. Follow-up cannot postpone required
atomic0040/0149 effects. Known incomplete valuation remains explicit on all current reports. Optional
coverage may remain Unassessed; known omissions/disputes cannot be hidden by declaration or closure.

## Historical evidence and correction routing

Classify late legitimate reporting, correction, new post-close work, unused-cancellation contradiction,
or unresolved evidence. Preserve occurrence precision/time, recording time, original evidence,
actor/authority, historical execution/hold context and dependent correction lineage. Recording does
not itself authorise execution, fulfil customer scope or recognise cost. Missing timestamps stay unknown.

Supported late evidence uses owning0146/0149 recognition/correction and0147 reassessment. Value-only
0040 adjustments change cost once, never physical quantity.0150 current-restated view changes while
closeout/source/saved report evidence remains. New post-close work requires separate supported
operational authority, never ordinary reopening or correction disguise. Unsupported quantity/ownership/
capacity effects stay unresolved and block applied correction, without unsafe partial downstream chain.

Unused-cancellation contradiction requires R7/Sales coordinated reconciliation and blocks unsafe claim
reuse; preserve actual report, do not delete it or automatically confiscate another Job's relied-on claim.
Closed discrepancy is a resolution case, not lifecycle restart. Finance controls later period/tax effects.

## Authority, consistency and refusals

Close authority is independent from source cost visibility and source execution/correction duties.
Owning adapter may expose a safe non-monetary readiness verdict with explicit security proof; never
rates/prices/history by implication. Current company/scope/operation checks apply in Request/Action,
history/export/snapshot/replay; restricted coordinator receives safe result or refusal, not hidden-cost
inference. Reuse R9/shared approvals as applicable; no new grant or universal independent reviewer.

Preflight readiness is advisory until commit revalidates relied-on evidence/generations. Prove compatible
Job/R7/source lock order and same runtime transaction participation, or verified generation protocol
covering concurrent actuals, holds, supplier corrections, custody and dispositions. Source-close races
must serialise or refuse stale evidence. Required owning effects/closeout receipt/evidence commit
together; no close then note. DB/blob storage is not one transaction; optional reports follow0137.

Stable company-scoped business/effect identity prevents duplicate disposition with fresh keys; exact
keyed intent replays only under current authority, changed intent conflicts. All relevant writers,
imports/corrections/workers included, participate in readiness/version protection. Missing adapter,
unknown impact, stale evidence, blocker or unsupported follow-up refuses affected closure/adjustment.
Exact lock/code/schema/API choices remain later verified delivery work, not implemented by this document.

## Acceptance scenarios — required, not executed tests

- Completion-only/no optional hours/cost/declaration: no fabricated entry or automatic complete zero.
- Known ordinary pending report/recognition: cannot reclassify as optional absence to close.
- Recognised unpriced actual: only real acknowledged owner/path/basis permits incomplete-value closeout.
- Installation9/10/custody3 or supplier60/10disputed/30remaining: owning disposition required.
- Hire period unknown or required source adapter absent: blocker/unknown, not N/A.
- Close races recognition/hold/correction/custody: coherent version and atomic outcome or refusal.
- Late value correction: one current adjustment, quantity/closeout/saved report preserved.
- Late quantity/unused-cancel contradiction: visible coordinated resolution, unsafe reuse blocked.
- New work disguised as correction: no work authority/fulfilment/recognition inferred.
- Follow-up owner unavailable: governed successor acknowledgement, no orphan or silent waiver.
- Retry/currently revoked or restricted actor: conflict/refusal/safe readiness, no duplicate effects/leak.
- Unsupported mandatory correction: no partially applied chain; original/current discrepancies retained.
