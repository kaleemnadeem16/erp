# Sales

The CRM foundation of Phase 4: optional leads, opportunities with one accountable owner, configurable OPEN stages, stable outcomes and the history behind them.
Quotations, pricing, tax, acceptance and job handover are **not** here yet — they wait for their accepted decisions (R2–R8).

## Owns

`Lead`, `Opportunity`, `SalesStage`, `SalesNote`, `SalesHistory` (the append-only decision log). A lead/opportunity points at a `Party` (and optionally one of its contacts); it never creates a customer account.

## Key actions

| Action | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `RecordLead`, `UpdateLead`, `LinkLeadParty`, `AssignLead`, `DecideLead` (`qualify` / `disqualify` / `revive`) | The optional enquiry, kept as received; every decision is a history row with its reason |
| `CreateOpportunity` | Direct, or converting a lead; `conversion_key` makes a retry return the deal already created; owner must be an active member |
| `UpdateOpportunity`, `ChangeOpportunityStage`, `ReassignOpportunity` | Keep the open deal current; stage names and owners are preserved in history |
| `CloseOpportunity`, `ReopenOpportunity` | LOST / ABANDONED with a reason; reopen the same pursuit. **WON is refused** until recorded customer acceptance exists |
| `AddSalesNote`, `MaintainSalesStages` | Authored notes; stage configuration (archive, never delete; the last active stage stays) |

## Internal financial and pricing kernel (Phase 4 batch B1)

Internal only — no route, table, permission, grant or UI; nothing here is reachable from HTTP. Contracts: [quotation-financial-calculation.md](../../../../docs/domain/quotation-financial-calculation.md) (FR0) and [sales-pricing-resolution.md](../../../../docs/domain/sales-pricing-resolution.md) (PR0).

- **`Support/Financial` (FR0).** `StorageAmount` is the decimal(19,4) adapter: non-negative, at most four places, never rounded silently. `Arithmetic` multiplies exactly (unit × quantity as a rational); `SettlementRule` (currency, `CurrencyQuantum` 0–4 places, an explicit `RoundingMode`) is the only way an amount is rounded; `SettledAmount` keeps the computed value, the settled amount and the residual apart; a header is `Arithmetic::sumSettled` of settled lines, never a re-rounded aggregate. `FinancialResult` is COMPLETE / PARTIAL / UNKNOWN and cannot be built inconsistently, so unknown never reads as zero and partial never as complete. The shared `MoneyCast` is **not** used and not changed: it loads a four-place amount in a two-place currency with `RoundingNecessaryException` (pinned by a characterisation test); it has no model callers today, and replacing it needs its own review.
- **`Support/Pricing` (PR0).** `ResolveSalesPrice` checks the Party (a prospect needs no CustomerAccount), verifies the source through a `PricingSourceAdapter` (direct Item and service Item, Assembly work from an ACTIVE version, governed manual scope), picks one `PriceCandidate` by explicit rank (no candidate, a currency mismatch or two equal best ranks refuse; never the cheapest) and settles the line. Cost comes read-only from `QueryMaterialCost` / the Estimating roll-up and is kept as certainty in its own currency; it never sets a price. Assembly work needs the existing `estimating.internal_cost.view` duty (a cost-blind adapter is a later slice). Alternate units and packages, the pricing master, FX, tax, discounts and controls are not here.
- **`Support/Fx` (FR1, [0118](../../../../docs/decisions/0118-pin-quotation-pricing-dates-and-company-base-fx.md)).** `ResolveQuotationFx` turns (source currency, quotation currency, `FinancialDate`, `FxPolicy`) into immutable `FxBundleEvidence` from the existing manual `ExchangeRate` master, SPOT only, this company only, writing nothing. Same currency is an IDENTITY route that reads no rate; otherwise a company-base bridge of source→base and quotation→base legs (a base-currency side is an explicit identity leg), with the exact ratio kept as a fraction and `convert()` settling through an FR0 `SettlementRule` into the quotation currency. `EXACT_DATE` (the built-in default) takes only a rate dated that day; `BOUNDED_PRIOR` takes, per leg, the latest rate on or before it within `maxAgeDays` (inclusive) and never a later one. Missing, too-old or non-positive rates refuse (`FX_RATE_MISSING`, `_TOO_OLD`, `_INVALID`) — never one or zero, no reciprocal, no direct pair, no other rate type. `attempt()` returns the same resolution as COMPLETE or UNKNOWN (naming each failing leg) for a caller that must report a figure as unknown rather than refuse; a conversion is never PARTIAL. `FinancialDate` is the company-local calendar date plus the UTC instant observed; `EffectivePeriod` is date-only half-open `[from, endExclusive)`. `FxPolicy` is a value with a built-in default: **there is no policy table and no evidence table yet** — the persisted, versioned policy waits for AU0/AU1, and the evidence row must carry R5's candidate/issue owner key, so `toArray()` and `fingerprint()` (stable under re-observation) are what that slice will store. Internal: no route, permission or UI, and it authorises nobody.
- **`Support/Financial/Policy` (FR4/PX6, [0120](../../../../docs/decisions/0120-settle-quotation-rounding-and-financial-evidence.md), [0144](../../../../docs/decisions/0144-version-sales-unit-price-precision-within-four-place-storage.md)).** `RoundingPolicy` is the immutable policy in force on a date: **unit-price entry precision** (two to four places, default four — `acceptUnitPrice` refuses a finer price with `UNIT_PRICE_PRECISION_EXCEEDED`, never rounds it) and the **rounding mode** (`SettlementRounding`: HALF_UP default, HALF_EVEN, HALF_DOWN, UP, DOWN) that `settlementRule($currency)` combines with the currency's own decimal places into an FR0 `SettlementRule`. The quantum is never stored in the policy. Line net settles to the currency quantum and the document net is the sum of settled lines; `SettlementEvidence` keeps the exact computed fraction, the settled amount and the signed residual. `fingerprint()` hashes the rules only (company, precision, mode, basis, calculation version — not the version number or dates), so `RoundingPolicyBinding` + `DraftRevalidation` can mark a draft stale and list prices that no longer fit without rewriting anything. Persistence is `sales_rounding_policy_versions` (company-composite keys, DRAFT → ACTIVE → SUPERSEDED, half-open periods with a database no-overlap constraint, immutable once activated, never deletable, closed only by its successor). `ResolveRoundingPolicy` answers for any date, history included, with the built-in default before the first activation. `CreateRoundingPolicyDraft` and `ActivateRoundingPolicy` are guarded by `sales.financial.configure`, which is **STAGED**: not registered and refused for everyone until a reviewed holder recipe activates it. No route, UI or grant. Aggregate tax rounding, residual allocation and the final-net controls are later FR4 slices; unit-price entry is not yet wired into pricing because no price-entry surface exists.
- **`Support/Financial/Allocation` (FR4 residual allocation, [0120](../../../../docs/decisions/0120-settle-quotation-rounding-and-financial-evidence.md)).** `AllocateFinancialResiduals` places an already-settled amount (a whole number of the currency's quanta, positive or negative) across `AllocationShare` components by the **largest-remainder** rule: each eligible component's exact share is total × weight ÷ Σ weights; the integer quanta are placed first and the quanta left over go one each to the largest fractional remainders, equal remainders ordered by the component's stable key (byte order), never by position. Ineligible components (exempt, not chargeable, unknown base — each with a reason) receive exactly zero and do not dilute the rest; a cap fixes a component and the remainder is divided again among the others, and an amount the eligible components cannot hold is refused. Integer and rational arithmetic only, nothing rounded; shares sum to the total exactly or the allocation is refused. `FinancialAllocation` is the evidence: the method, tie policy, quantum, and per component the weight, cap, exact raw share, allocated amount, signed residual and whether it took an extra quantum, in canonical key order with a `fingerprint()` that is the same for the same inputs however they were ordered. Pure and persistence-free; it keeps the allocated contribution distinct from each line's computed base (3 × 0.0100 at FX 0.333: computed bases 0.0099, header 0.0100, allocated 0.0034/0.0033/0.0033, residual 0.0001). Aggregate tax rounding, final-net controls and any storage of the result are later slices.
- **`Support/Financial/Controls` (FR4 final-net controls, [0116](../../../../docs/decisions/0116-govern-sales-discounts-and-final-net-controls.md), [0120](../../../../docs/decisions/0120-settle-quotation-rounding-and-financial-evidence.md)).** `EvaluateSettledNetControls` judges a line's final settled net (`SettledNetFacts`: net, exact priced quantity, cost as FR0 certainty, governed reference, discretionary input, pinned FX evidence, rounding policy) against a company's `NetControlSet`. Seven typed controls, advisory or mandatory, each with its own limit: floor; minimum margin as an amount or a percentage of net; the specific discretionary input and the aggregate reduction (reference − net), each as a percentage or an amount of the governed reference. Percentage and amount limits are separate controls; nothing is clamped. Outcomes: **PASS**; **WARN** (advisory violated); **FAIL** (mandatory violated); **UNEVALUABLE** (evidence missing, unknown, partial, incompatible — never a pass, never zero; a COMPLETE zero cost is real; percentage margin at zero net is undefined). Overall precedence FAIL > UNEVALUABLE > WARN > PASS; `blocksIssue()` is any mandatory FAIL or UNEVALUABLE, and an advisory UNEVALUABLE asks for review without blocking. Exact rationals only, at-the-limit passes, per-unit amounts × the exact quantity, another currency only through pinned FX bundles for the pricing date and company (no reciprocal), controls written for another rounding policy are UNEVALUABLE. No actor or delegate ceilings and no bypass: an effective limit is resolved elsewhere (R9) into a control. `consumerSafe()` returns only opaque control refs, outcomes and the blocking flag — no cost, margin, threshold, reference, reason, kind or fingerprint; `toInternalArray()` and `fingerprint()` carry the operands and are server-only. `ControlEvidenceProvider` is the seam for an authorised cost-blind source; only `UnavailableControlEvidence` (always UNKNOWN) exists, so no real cost-blind adapter, route or grant is delivered.
- **Quotation foundation (QL0, [record](../../../../docs/phases/phase-4-ql0-delivery-and-decisions.md)).** `quotation_families` (stable business quotation, one optional current CRM association with an append-only, chained `quotation_family_association_events` history), `quotation_revisions` (explicit revisions, database-allocated ordinal, recipient Party, one currency, dates, predecessor; `DRAFT` or `CANCELLED_DRAFT` only, input generation advances with every material change), `quotation_logical_scopes` and `quotation_choice_groups` (family-level identities that survive revisions — a scope is an intended deliverable, never an Item or Assembly id; alternatives share an overlap anchor and a choose-one group has one baseline) and `quotation_draft_lines` (customer-facing lines typed DIRECT_ITEM / SERVICE_ITEM / ASSEMBLY_WORK / MANUAL, written only on a draft revision, offered quantity exact at four places). Tenancy is composite keys throughout; the database also enforces source integrity, immutable cancelled drafts and identity. **At QL0 no price, cost, tax, number, issue state, route, Action or permission existed**; QL1a (below) adds the draft Actions and entered money, while ISSUED, candidates and issue receipts still arrive with the slices that can supply their evidence. `QuotationNumbering` is registered but nothing consumes it. A draft line maps to the pricing kernel with `QuotationDraftLine::pricingSource()`.
- **Quotation draft preparation (QL1a, backend only, [QL0 record §9](../../../../docs/phases/phase-4-ql0-delivery-and-decisions.md)).** Four guarded Actions, no route, controller, screen or client change: `CreateQuotationDraft` (family plus first draft revision; currency defaults to the company base; an optional Opportunity link needs `prepare` and an independent pipeline read and is recorded as the first association event), `UpdateQuotationDraft` (typed header fields and lines to add, change or remove, in one transaction, with the caller's expected input generation — a moved draft is `QUOTATION_DRAFT_STALE`), `PreviewQuotation` (read-only: each line through PR0 source resolution, the FR4 policy on the pricing date and FR1 evidence; document net is the sum of settled lines) and `ReadQuotationDraft` (safe metadata only). Both writers run through `Support/Quotation/QuotationOperation`: one append-only receipt per (company, actor, key) in `sales_quotation_operation_receipts`; the same key and intent replays the original result before any freshness check, a different intent under it is `REQUEST_KEY_REUSED`. **Entered money** is a seller's unit price in the quotation currency stored only together with its pinned FX evidence and base-currency unit price (`QuotationMoney`); a missing rate refuses that money write while non-money edits continue, and the currency cannot change under priced lines (database trigger as well). A price of exactly zero is a known zero; no price is NULL and the line net is UNKNOWN. The revision remembers the rounding policy its prices were prepared under so `DraftRevalidation` can flag a change. **Measures** (`priced_*`, `stock_*`, `conversion_version`) are copied from the PR0 adapter: only the Item's own stock unit is supported, so the conversion version is 1 (identity) and package or alternate units refuse. Only DIRECT_ITEM and MANUAL lines are authorised; service Item, Assembly and any estimating or cost evidence are not, and no cost is read into or returned by any result. **Tax is UNKNOWN** (FR2/FR3 are not delivered), final-net controls report `evaluated: 0` (no control set or cost-blind adapter exists) and `issue_ready` is always false. A draft line with an entered price registers a `FinancialActivity` probe, so the company base currency locks. `sales.quotation.view` (`ReadQuotationDraft`) and `sales.quotation.prepare` (the other three) are **STAGED**: not registered, granted to nobody, refused by `SalesAccess` until a reviewed holder recipe activates them.
- **Quotation ownership (QA1a, backend only, issue #59; [proposal on #50](https://github.com/kaleemnadeem16/erp/issues/50); design in issue #45).** `quotation_families` gain `responsible_owner_id` (current accountability; `created_by` stays the immutable provenance and is never copied into it), a reserved nullable `access_unit_id` (a typed seam: no unit is inferred and nothing reads it for visibility), `ownership_generation`, `access_generation` and `access_baseline_state`: **UNRESOLVED** (every existing row; no owner, no unit, generations 0 — there is no backfill), **NATIVE** (created with its owner on record) or **CAPTURED** (adopted from an approved mapping). `quotation_ownership_events` is the append-only chain (INITIAL or CAPTURED_BASELINE first, then REASSIGNED and UNIT_CHANGED), with composite company keys, old and new values, actor, reason and the database's own clock; the database refuses UPDATE, DELETE and TRUNCATE, enforces the chain, and at COMMIT requires the family to equal its latest event. `CreateQuotationDraft` now records the creator as the first owner with an INITIAL event in the same transaction. `ReassignQuotationOwner`, `ChangeQuotationAccessUnit` and the INTERNAL `CaptureQuotationAccessBaseline` (no route, command or migration calls it) take the caller's expected ownership and access generations, a reason and a request key (replay before staleness, a different intent under a key is refused); naming the current owner or unit records nothing. They need `sales.quotation.assign` AND an independent `sales.quotation.view` through `SalesAccess` — both STAGED, so every caller is refused, and a restricted grant is refused outright: no scoped OWN visibility exists until QA1b. Owners must be active company members; a unit must be an active unit of the company; ownership grants the new owner nothing. Excluded: shared RecordScope/Administration changes, teams, delegation, lists, routes, UI, grants and activation.
- **`Support/Kernel` (AU2/AU5 interfaces).** `SalesActorContext` (explicit actor and company, identity only — not a permission verdict), the `OperationReceiptPort` interface and `OperationIntent::hash` (the existing CRM canonical-hash convention). No receipt table or implementation.

**Quantities (PX1, [0142](../../../../docs/decisions/0142-represent-stock-quantities-exactly-and-supersede-0050-increment-validation.md)):** there is no universal scale. `PricingInput` keeps the requested quantity untouched and each source adapter proves it with the shared `App\Support\Quantity` contract (`Pricing/PricingQuantity`) *before* any cost read: direct and service Items are four ledger places and a whole multiple of the Item's own `quantity_precision`; Assembly work is the six-place technical scale; governed manual scope is four places. Refusals carry the shared codes (`QUANTITY_INVALID`, `_NEGATIVE`, `_ZERO`, `_OVERFLOW`, `_NOT_REPRESENTABLE`, `_INCREMENT_VIOLATED`); nothing is rounded, and `ResolvedSource::$quantity` is the proven value used for cost and the line. The six-place Assembly scale bounds the *technical* measure only — the scale an Assembly may be *offered* at on a quotation is still open (R2/R5) and must be refused, not assumed, when that slice arrives. `Arithmetic::quantity` is only the widest-destination ceiling (nine places) for the arithmetic itself.

## Duty manifest and registration (Phase 4 AU0/AU1)

`Support/Authority/SalesDutyManifest` is the one source of the Sales permission vocabulary. `SalesDutyCatalogue` lists the 58 Sales-owned rows of the accepted R9 matrix with a class (E, E-S, N, S, D-S) and a status. **Only the eight existing CRM duties are ACTIVE**; the other 50 are SPECIFIED (44), STAGED (1: `sales.financial.configure`, whose guarded Action exists but is disabled) or DORMANT (5): not registered, no constant, no permission row, and `SalesAccess::may` refuses them even if a row is made by hand. Registration (`registerWith`) is idempotent and grants nothing; every ACTIVE duty that is not an existing non-sensitive CRM duty is excluded from automatic grants, so a new duty — ordinary or sensitive — reaches only an explicit holder or a verified full-access mandate. A duty goes live only in the change that delivers its Action, guard, projection and reviewed holder recipe, by moving its status in the catalogue and adding its constant. Scope is company-wide only; a "my units" limit refuses (403). Other modules' duties (Documents, Tax, Company, Currency, …) are not registered here. Per-duty rollout detail: [phase-4-au0-au1-rollout-manifest.md](../../../../docs/phases/phase-4-au0-au1-rollout-manifest.md).

A guarded Action must check its own duty and scope before calling the kernel and project the result with `PricingResult::forReader`.

## Non-obvious

- **Access.** `SalesAccess` is the one guard: the capability *and* "not limited to my units". Own/assigned/unit visibility is not built, so a limited person is **refused (403)**, never shown a filtered list (ADR 0106). Ownership grants nothing.
- **A closed opportunity is immutable** until reopened (database trigger); `sales_events` and `sales_notes` are append-only; stages and leads cannot be deleted.
- **Unknown value stays unknown**: `expected_amount` and `expected_currency_id` are both set or both null.
- **Numbering** (`LEAD`, `OPPORTUNITY`) is allocated when the record is created; the format is managed with `sales.numbering.manage`.
- Parties' candidate search lets Sales users (`sales.lead.manage`, `sales.opportunity.manage`) ask whether an identity already exists.

## Related

- **Quotation draft review, read adapters (QL1b-1, [#30](https://github.com/kaleemnadeem16/erp/issues/30)).** `GET quotations/{revision}` (`QuotationController::show` -> `ReadQuotationDraft`, `sales.quotation.view`) and `GET quotations/{revision}/preview` (`preview` -> `PreviewQuotation`, `sales.quotation.prepare`): Request guard plus the Action's own guard, Resources pass the Actions' safe projections through unchanged. Both duties are still **STAGED**, so every caller, administrators and full-access mandate holders included, gets 403; nothing is activated and no holder recipe is applied. A view holder cannot preview, a prepare holder cannot read metadata (independent duties). Another company's revision, like an unknown id, is a 404 (looked up through the company-scoped model after the capability guard), never data.
- **Quotation draft write adapters (QL1b-2, [#53](https://github.com/kaleemnadeem16/erp/issues/53)).** `POST quotations` (`QuotationController::store` -> `CreateQuotationDraft`) and `PATCH quotations/{revision}` (`update` -> `UpdateQuotationDraft`, body field `expected_generation` required), both `sales.quotation.prepare` in the Request and again in the Action, and both still **STAGED**: every caller gets 403, nothing is activated, no holder recipe applied. The `Idempotency-Key` header is the Action's request key (required, at most 85 characters); an exact retry replays the stored result (200, `Idempotency-Replayed: true`), a first create is 201. The body must be a JSON object (malformed JSON, a scalar or a list is a 422 on `body`, never an empty draft); its shape is declared in the Request rules so the OpenAPI contract and generated client are typed (`Idempotency-Key` is a declared required header, passed as the client's typed `headers` argument; `frontend/packages/core-api/src/contracts/quotation-draft-writes.check.ts` is the compile-time contract), but the rules only check types and formats, and the raw JSON goes to the Action untouched, so an unknown header or line key is refused (422 `INVALID_INPUT`), never ignored; the response carries only `family_id`, `revision_id`, `input_generation` and `replayed`, no figure. Statuses (`Support/Quotation/QuotationRefusalStatus`, tested): **404** a foreign or unknown revision (same body as an unknown id); **409** only `REQUEST_KEY_REUSED`; **422** with its `reason_code` for every other refusal, stale evidence (`QUOTATION_DRAFT_STALE`) and a draft that is not editable included, per api-conventions. Not here: list/search, UI, issuance, record-level access (company-wide guard until the access slices), activation.
- **Issue readiness (QL3 groundwork, branch `phase4/ql3-issue-readiness`).** `Actions/AssessQuotationIssueReadiness` answers "could this draft be issued today?" as a list of named blockers — codes, a scope (`HEADER` / `LINE` / `TAX` / `CONTROLS` / `AUTHORITY`) and an optional line id; never an amount, cost, rate or free text. Guarded by `sales.quotation.prepare` (STAGED, limited scope refused), read-only, allocates no number. It checks the header (recipient present and active, pricing date, a known valid-through date that is not in the past, at least one line), every line through the same PR0/FR4 preview the preparer already sees (price entered, net complete, the pinned FX evidence still the same, the rounding policy the prices were prepared under still current), and then two independent evidence checks behind interfaces: `TaxIssueCheck` (Tax-owned FR3 plugs in by rebinding; default `UndeterminedTaxIssueCheck` -> `TAX_NOT_DETERMINED`) and `ControlsIssueCheck` (a configured control set and cost evidence; default `UnconfiguredControlsIssueCheck` -> `CONTROLS_NOT_CONFIGURED`). Unknown is never a pass. `ISSUE_NOT_AVAILABLE` is reported while `Actions\IssueQuotation` does not exist and disappears by itself when it does. No route, no schema, no state change, no numbering; issue itself (ISSUED state, immutable snapshot, official number, receipt) is the next slice.
- **Issue storage (QL3, ADR 0123; storage only — `IssueQuotation` follows).** `quotation_revisions` gains the ISSUED state with `official_number`, `numbering_allocation_id`, `issued_at` (the database's transaction time), `issued_by`, the frozen `issued_timezone` and the exact `expires_at`; `quotation_issues` holds exactly one append-only row per issued revision (the reviewed input generation, the maker = the revision's creator and the checker = the issuer, the pinned rounding / tax / controls evidence references, the settled document totals and a fingerprint of the frozen lines, `unknown_payment_policy` = BLOCK) and `quotation_issued_lines` the frozen settled lines (net, tax, gross, disposition, opaque Tax evidence reference; never a rate, profile fact or cost). The database refuses any change to an issued revision, any draft-line write once it is issued and any update, delete or TRUNCATE of the issue record, and at COMMIT it checks that the issue is whole: the revision, its issue row, the Numbering allocation (company, `QUOTATION`, this revision, this number), the frozen lines (exactly the draft's lines) and the totals (sums of the lines that count) all agree. One number per revision and per company. Rolling back refuses while any issue exists. No Action, route or permission yet: nothing can write these tables.
- **Quotation document binding (DOC0-Sales skeleton, [ADR 0137](../../../../docs/decisions/0137-share-document-and-print-infrastructure-through-module-adapters.md), [Documents README](../Documents/README.md)).** Sales owns `quotation_customer_document_snapshots` (the frozen customer projection of a revision: schema version, projection, digest; append-only; composite FK to the revision) and `quotation_document_bindings` (the real FKs Documents does not hold — to the snapshot and to `document_subject_bindings`; append-only; a database trigger requires the Documents identity to be type `sales.quotation.customer`, the same audience, owner identity `quotation-snapshot:<id>` and generation 1). `Support/Documents/QuotationCustomerDocumentAdapter` declares the customer-safe projection allowlist (no cost, margin, control or internal note field exists to carry) and the access callback, which asks `SalesAccess` for `sales.document.generate`; `QuotationDocumentProvider` is registered with `DocumentTypeRegistry` from this module's provider. `BindQuotationDocument` is **internal with no caller**: inside Sales' transaction it calls `BindDocumentSubject` (idempotent, no second archive identity) and records the Sales row. `sales.document.generate` is **STAGED** (refused for everyone, not registered, not granted). **Nothing writes a snapshot yet**: freezing from a real issued quotation is QL3/DF2, and that writer must compute and verify `projection_digest` itself and require an ISSUED revision (the table trusts neither). Free text in the projection is `customer_notes`. No route, issue, numbering, template or renderer.
