## Practical examples

**Simple visit:** supported service outcome is complete; no hours were required/captured. An evidenced
labour15000.0000 is fully valued; optional overall labour coverage remains Unassessed. No known pending
work/source obligation exists. Recommend close without inventing hours or a declaration, retaining
unassessed coverage. If a known performed expense is awaiting ordinary recognition, resolve it first;
optional absence is different from an identified unresolved source.

**Installation:** need10, transfer12, consume9, retain3 in vehicle. Completion of all10 cannot be
claimed. Either finish supported remainder or stop and coordinate its supported successor/Sales
disposition. Transfer/reallocate unused3 through Inventory; naming a custodian does not itself remove
the Job custody obligation. Subcontract60 accepted/10 disputed/30 unperformed needs owning resolution,
not a 'partially done' percentage. Supplier commitments do not vanish when Job execution stops.

**Valuation follow-up:** accepted/evidenced supplier work has an authorised supported source, but final
invoice amount is missing. Closeout may follow0145 only when operational facts are resolved and the
valuation gap has explicit source/reason, accountable owner and delivered correction path. Later
invoice confirms/reconciles under0149; current cost changes, original closeout/report stays historical.
This is not permission to close an unknown supplier-performance dispute or bypass missing adapters.

## Source-specific closeout assessment — recommended refinement

Use one bounded reconciliation result per applicable source family: **Resolved**, **Supported valuation
follow-up**, **Blocking obligation/discrepancy**, **Unsupported/unknown**, or **Not applicable with evidence**.
These are proposed closeout-check outcomes, not lifecycle/cost states. No manually asserted N/A for a
known source. Each owner reports source scope/generation, facts checked, remaining effects and receipt.

| Source/fact | Must resolve before close | May remain only under governed valuation follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| Operational work/customer remainder | Outcome evidence or actual supported successor/reduction; capacity/lineage conserved | None merely because labour plan unused |
| Supplier PO/service/subcontract | Unperformed/rejected/disputed quantities, remaining commitment and cancellation/short-close effects; supported obligation transfer if any | Supported later price/invoice correction of already resolved performance, not unknown claim |
| Hire | Off-hire/charge period/custody responsibility and known outstanding operational charges | Supported final valuation adjustment on established period/basis |
| Inventory custody/material demand | Actual transfer/reallocation/return and demand disposition; supported reservation effects if applicable | Valuation-only0040 follow-up with physical facts resolved and correction participant delivered |
| Labour/external actuals | Known pending ordinary evidence/recognition, historical context/hold-work review and quantity/attribution discrepancy | Recognised supported actual with identified missing valuation and real correction owner/path |
| Required correction | Quantity/ownership/allocation correction or unknown downstream impact | Identified value-only correction only where accepted protocol safely supports pending follow-up; not blanket permission to defer mandatory atomic effects |
| Coverage | Reconcile known omissions/disputes; retain current0147 status | Optional Unassessed/Declared Complete stays honestly labelled, never auto-confirmed |

An unfinished owning operation is not a disposition. For stock kept in a vehicle, a supported transfer
of operational responsibility/custody linkage can discharge this Job without moving stock to origin;
otherwise the Job remains accountable. No parallel balance, forced site setup or general consumed-return
adapter is introduced. Explicit reservations remain deferred; do not invent release effects in v1.

## Meaningful choices and recommendations

**C1 — evidence-based closeout checks, not a universal checklist ceremony.** Recommend derive applicable
source families and blockers from authoritative inventory of writers/source references, plus owning
verification. SIMPLE sees concise unresolved facts; only affected owners act. Alternative manual
checkboxes are easy but cannot prove source absence or pending effects. Universal independent approval
adds burden; reuse existing duties/configured review instead. A no-source assertion must have a real
supported source inventory, not an unavailable adapter defaulting to NONE.

**C2 — bounded follow-up responsibility.** Recommend follow-up retains source/component, missing fact,
reason, estimated/unknown amount basis, accountable eligible person/team, owning module/action/path,
expected review date or explicit event trigger, authority and acknowledgement. Reassignment requires
eligible successor acknowledgement/history; no orphaned follow-up on deactivation. Suggested review
date tracks responsibility, not an automatic closeout waiver or arbitrary financial deadline.
Alternative block all missing final prices would wrongly make operational closeout depend on Finance.
Do not accept a reason-only note when owning effects/operational obligations remain unknown.

**C3 — disputes.** Recommend conservative first scope: unresolved performed quantity, rejection,
chargeable period, ownership or allocation blocks. A monetary difference is eligible follow-up only
after source owner proves performance/scope resolved and an explicit supported valuation/dispute path;
otherwise block. No broad 'all disputes can remain' rule. Owner review needed on the exact later
valuation-dispute adapter, not a change to0145's quantity/discrepancy guards.

**C4 — post-close evidence resolution.** Classify late legitimate report, correction of existing fact,
new work after close, unused-cancellation contradiction, or unresolved evidence. Preserve occurrence
and recording time/authority, original references and supporting history. Capture truth without
auto-authorising, recognising or fulfilling work. New post-close work cannot masquerade as correction;
requires separate eligible operational scope. Known unsupported discrepancy remains visible as a
resolution case, not silently discarded or an ordinary reopen. No general correction bypass duty.

## Historical handling examples and safeguards

- Late pre-close labour expense: verify historical execution/context and overlap, apply0146 recognition/
  correction where supported, reassess0147 and restate0150 current view. Preserve closeout evidence.
- Late0040 material valuation: source-owned append adjustment once, physical quantity unchanged.
- Correction of accepted quantity: reconcile affected supplier/customer/source allocations through
  owning actions. If unsupported, retain discrepancy and refuse applied correction; no unsafe partial chain.
- Evidence disproves CANCELLED_UNUSED: preserve report, open coordinated reconciliation and block unsafe
  claim reuse. Do not delete actuals to keep cancellation eligibility or automatically take capacity
  already relied on by another Job. Supported Sales/R7 correction must resolve conflicting claims.
- Evidence date lacks detail: do not fabricate timestamp to pass historical hold check. Governed review
  must classify uncertainty; general late-entry access does not permit unauthorised held execution.

Current scope/operation/cost permissions apply on every read/write/retry. The closeout actor needs
owning results, not unrestricted warehouse/supplier/worker/price access; restricted projections may show
safe blockers or refuse, without leaking costs through counts/details. Whether a source can return a
safe non-monetary readiness verdict needs its explicit adapter/security proof, not implicit broad access.

## What must be delivered together, and safe refusal

Closeout preflight collects dated source generations/obligation receipts; commit rechecks under compatible
Job/R7/source locks or a proven transactional generation protocol. A preflight screen is not authority.
Source recognition/correction, cancel/short-close, custody/demand reassignment and closeout writers must
participate so concurrent new evidence cannot slip past closure. Exact implementation remains later.
Atomically persist required dispositions, Job closeout receipt and relied-on evidence on verified
connections. Missing mandatory participant/unknown impact → refuse; never partial close plus note.

Supported valuation follow-up does not postpone a mandatory atomic correction effect.0148/0040 and0149
activation gates stand before source enablement. Pure read-only/reporting work cannot discharge a
supplier or stock obligation. An unsupported optional future category does not universally block an
unrelated supported pilot, but a known affected obligation cannot be hidden by calling it optional.

Source-family applicability/readiness, exact-intent closeout receipt, historical adjustment routing,
coverage invalidation and current cost-view reconciliation must be specified together for each enabled
pilot path. Durable report artefacts require0137 adapter/security; no mandatory PDF/Finance rollout to
close a supported Job with an authoritative closeout record. Real holders/configuration remain separate.
