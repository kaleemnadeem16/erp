## C4 — Distinguish four amounts before calculating a partial

1. **Original issued amount/allocation:** immutable historical attribution for original offered scope.
2. **Original entitlement consumed:** only what the customer is entitled to under supported unchanged
   partial terms; cumulative benefits/component shares cannot exceed their original assessed budget.
3. **New agreed commercial amount:** reoffer has separately authorised price/benefit/condition facts
   and fresh customer consent; it is not an edit to original allocation or already accepted value.
4. **Later legal tax/settlement amount:** Finance/localisation uses its legal tax point/assessment and
   settlement facts, with links/variances; neither old attribution nor new quotation is a tax return.

### Exact residual example: why independently prorated amounts fail

Original settled benefit0.0300 applies to four divisible units. Customer accepts1, then1, then2.
Assume an explicitly supported proportional original-scope entitlement and HALF_UP quantum0.01:

| Accepted slice | Independent raw share | Naïve rounded share | Cumulative accepted quantity | Settled cumulative entitlement | Increment actually consumed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
|1/4 |0.0075 |0.0100 |1/4 |0.0100 |0.0100 |
|1/4 |0.0075 |0.0100 |2/4 |0.0200 |0.0100 |
|2/4 |0.0150 |0.0200 |4/4 |0.0300 |0.0100 |
|Total |0.0300 |**0.0400** |4/4 |0.0300 |**0.0300** |

Recommended supported partial policy: `E(q)=round(original_budget × cumulative_q / eligible_q)`;
new share=`E(new cumulative_q) − already_consumed`, with original cap/non-negative checks. This
stage follows original R4 line/group allocation; it does not replace largest-remainder allocation
across original lines. Version method/quantum, retain exact input quantities and deterministic original
scope/line tie order. No caller chooses the residual by replay order or invents new scope IDs.
Cumulative order can affect which slice receives a quantum; show that in actual accepted evidence.
Use authoritative serial claim order, not retroactive sorting by a late claimed occurrence time. Replay
doesn't advance it. Customer consent must cover the settled amount/declared rounding terms; if a race
changes that agreed result, refuse for refreshed review/consent rather than silently changing a cent.

The same arithmetic illustrates the danger for a tax-component allocation **only if its assessed
scope permits proportional allocation**. It is not a legal prorating rule. Fixed duties, minimums,
exemptions and graph dependencies must use their supported assessment adapter. Final net/margin/floor
checks consume settled allocated net on compatible quantity and complete applicable cost when required;
never silently clamp amounts or invent cost/tolerance to force a pass.

Cumulative acceptance and allocated benefit are independent ledgers, updated atomically. Retrying1/4
must not advance either. A different legitimate decision can consume another1/4; a different retry key
for the same decision cannot. Correction uses a linked governed adjustment, not rewriting the prior share.

## Multi-component/fixed-tax companion — allocation isn't new assessment

Original net800 with A10% NET=80, B5% NET_PLUS(A)=44 and one fixed component F20 gives gross944.
A historical40% attribution is net320/A32/B17.6000/F8, gross377.6000;60% is480/48/26.4000/12,
gross566.4000. The two historical allocations sum944, preserving each original component budget.
For additive B5% NET instead, B=40 and original gross940: composition matters, not only summed rates.

**F8 is not automatically the fixed tax payable for a newly agreed40 transaction.** If a supported
configured assessment legitimately applies F20 to an independently offered40 transaction, its new
net360/A36/B19.8000/F20 yields gross435.8000. Reissuing that same transaction doesn't itself justify
another F20; another independently assessed transaction must have genuine declared basis. If legal
partial assessment is unknown, do not invent8 or20 from document numbering: obtain supported facts/
reoffer or keep disputed/unsupported acceptance pending as appropriate. Finance determines actual
invoice legal tax later, linked to original/new quote estimates and explicit variances.

Multiple additive/compounded components retain named dependency and raw/settled stages, component
exemptions and effective versions. Round/allocate by supported policy with per-component conservation;
never pool different rates/jurisdictions/dispositions or let exempt/unknown lines absorb residuals.
Original allocation totals and newly assessed component totals have different identities: do not
force the new transaction to an old budget, or count original and successor assessments as two sales.

Original transaction/base conversions also stay immutable. For three0.0100 amounts at pinned0.333,
computed base4 values0.0033 each sum0.0099 versus converted header0.0100. Keep separately allocated
contribution/residual0.0001, not overwrite computed base to balance. New reoffer may have different
explicit financial dates/FX; reporting/settlement FX are separate, not live rewrites of old consent.
