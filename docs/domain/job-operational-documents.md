# Job operational documents and reporting outputs

**2026-10-16 · ACCEPTED0152/Q57 resolved; bounded contract SPECIFIED, not implemented/verified.**
Live operational evidence differs from formal generation;0137 remains shared infrastructure owner.

## Ownership and first-pilot boundary

Job/source business records → current-authorised live view OR genuinely required frozen profile →
Documents manifest/intent/render/private original. No new universal document/Job lifecycle.
Job owns meaning/audience/snapshot; sources supply authorised facts; Company owns branding/identity;
Documents owns rendering/configuration/storage/recovery/reprints. No duplicate archive or renderer.

Required: live current worksheet, durable supported completion/0151 closeout evidence and supported
operational/0150 cost views with authority/completeness labels. Deferred optional: formal customer
report, downloadable immutable cost artefact, signatures/consent, work packs/archives. No compulsory
PDF. A path requiring formal output needs actual0137 and Job adapter. Upstream sources retain their
own requirements; optional Job PDFs do not waive mandatory upstream source snapshots.

## Content and logical evidence — no physical schema

Live worksheet: company/Job identity, authorised scope/activity/instructions, assignment/schedule,
baseline/plan version, as-of and effective hold/termination/restrictions. Printed/saved sheets identify
their version/as-of, never authorise work against current restrictions. Offline authority/sync deferred.

Completion/closeout records retain actual outcome/remainder, actor/time and supported evidence/readiness
references under0145/0151. Formal completion output binds exact supported outcome revision and audience;
generation/download/signature line neither completes work nor establishes customer consent.
Internal cost output uses0150 contributions/corrections, separate commitments/exposure, provisional/
unpriced distinctions, coverage, cutoffs/generations and baseline. Missing is not zero or final margin.

Customer-safe/internal-operation/internal-cost profiles have distinct authorised projections/identities.
No full internal payload with hidden sections. Freeze only where required: business facts/profile/
source generations plus0137 template/brand/assets/page/format/schema/renderer context and durable intent.
Optional later capture records its actual basis/time, not fictitious completion-time evidence. Required
manifest/intent binds atomically with owning snapshot event; render later outside long transactions.
Optional generation failure does not undo completion; mandatory bind failure follows exact affected
owner contract. DB/blob storage is not one transaction.

## Pages, reprints and historical corrections

A4 preferred; individually verify other profiles. Long activities/notes/evidence paginate without
clipping/dropped rows/duplicate totals/misleading repeats. Repeat identity/page/version/as-of safely for
audience. Pin original template/brand/assets/version; later settings cannot rewrite history.
Reprint original bytes after current access. Changed meaning uses owning correction and linked successor;
layout-only repair is labelled rendition under0137 equivalence checks.0150/0151 history persists;
rendering confers neither ordinary reopening nor arbitrary historical reconstruction.

Attachments use private validation/quarantine and immutable relied-on versions with subject/audience
associations. No automatic universal retention/purge/public links; owner retention/holds govern.

## Authority, integrity and delivery gates

Guard Request and Action for company/subject/source/operation/audience, including live/preview/history/
status/download/reprint/cache/replay/attachments. Assignment, file possession, configuration/recovery
rights grant no business read. Restricted readers get approved narrower profile or refusal; no hidden
cost inference from totals/metadata/pages/hashes. Current access applies to saved originals too.
No redundant generic download grant; reviewed exact duty/holder rollout is delivery work.

0137 owns typed tenant-safe binding, exact intent/receipts, private originals, fenced workers/recovery.
Prove compatible owning transactions/locks and source-snapshot consistency. Fresh-key same generation
resolves original; changed intent conflicts. Reports/rebuilds never repost economic sources. Missing
mandatory adapter refuses. Preserve DF0–DF5 sequence; registration/PO Quick Print does not prove delivery.

## Acceptance scenarios — required, not executed tests

- Worksheet reflects restrictions; old paper cannot start held/cancelled work.
- Completion/closeout without optional PDF/hours: no invented evidence/extra approval.
- Customer projection/bytes/metadata contain no internal financial operands or private attachments.
- Partial/stopped report truthful; generation is not acceptance or fulfilment.
- Cost view labels provisional/unpriced/coverage/cutoffs without false complete total/margin.
- Capture races correction: one pinned generation or stale refusal; no repost/mixed snapshot.
- Reprint after brand/source change: original bytes; changed meaning is linked successor.
- Optional generation failure preserves outcome; required bind failure respects exact owner contract.
- Revoked/cross-company/restricted/cache access denied; actual multipage/worker/storage guarantees tested
  when formal adapter delivered, not claimed verified by planning.

## Landmines

PDF absence is not missing business evidence. Printed instructions are not current work authority.
Browser print/type registration is not delivered formal document infrastructure.
