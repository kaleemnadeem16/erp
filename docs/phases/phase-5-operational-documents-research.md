# Phase 5 — operational documents, reports and snapshots

**2026-10-16 · PROPOSED Q57 research/decision bundle. No new document architecture accepted.**
Question: which minimal outputs help service execution, and when must authoritative business evidence
be pinned into a protected document under0137 without another renderer or universal document lifecycle?

## Practical examples before contracts

**Small repair business:** dispatcher opens an authorised Job work sheet showing location, contact,
scope, access instructions and assignment. Technician completes supported outcome evidence without
hours/cost capture. Owner optionally shares a concise completion report with the customer. Job completion
does not wait for a PDF unless that specific governing workflow genuinely requires it. No copied rates,
supplier prices or optional internal notes appear in the customer report.

**Managed installation:** multiple activities have versioned work instructions. A printed sheet clearly
states its plan version/as-of date and 'check current authority before work'; stale paper cannot authorise
held/stopped work. A completion report captures only evidenced fulfilled scope, with partial/stopped
outcome and remainder correctly described. Internal cost summary separately includes recognised known
cost, provisional/unpriced gaps, commitments and coverage under0150. Customer report omits this data
at projection creation, not by visually hiding columns from a full internal PDF.

**Later correction:** invoice adjustment changes current authorised cost view. Saved cost summary dated
at closeout remains the original snapshot; a new corrected summary is a linked successor with new
as-of/source generations. It is not a reprint of changed bytes. A technical layout repair is a labelled
rendition only if owner proves unchanged business meaning. New outcome evidence may require owning
historical reconciliation0151; Documents cannot edit completion truth or grant work authority.

## Settled infrastructure reused — sufficient existing research

0137 and [Documents](../domain/documents.md) already settle shared typed adapters, Company branding,
versioned template/page policy, manifests/durable intents, isolated rendering, private artefacts,
exact-byte reprints, current owner-authorised downloads and recovery. Reuse its
[primary comparison](../architecture/document-print-framework-review.md) (Frappe printing/letterhead,
Business Central dataset/layout/company defaults; accessed2026-10-15) rather than repeat vendor search.
They separate content/dataset from common layout/configuration; their scripting/Word/RDLC choices
do not justify another runtime or universal issue/approval lifecycle here.

Shared framework remains ACCEPTED/SPECIFIED, not delivered. Its quotation-first DF0–DF5 sequence and
PO-shaped fixture are not reordered or expanded by this research. A future Job adapter can reuse the
verified framework; type registration alone does not prove Job snapshot/access/retention support.
No PDF library installation, independent archive or live PO print refactor is proposed.

## Smallest useful outputs and pilot boundary — recommendations

| Output | Required content / owner | First service-pilot recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Live Job work sheet/view | Job: identity, authorised scope/activity/plan revision, current effective hold/start restrictions, assignment/schedule, site/contact/instructions with scope controls | Useful minimal execution view; no compulsory formal PDF or separate issued-document state |
| Durable completion/closeout evidence | Job/source owners: actual supported outcome/remainder, actor/time/source evidence;0151 closeout readiness/follow-ups | Mandatory business record for the affected transition; a generated document is not a substitute |
| Customer completion report | Job adapter: truthful full/partial/stopped scope, occurrence/report dates, approved customer-safe outcome/evidence profile; no inferred consent | Optional first pilot output; deliver later unless actual customer/governing policy requires it |
| Internal authorised cost summary | Job costing adapter:0150 measures/source generations/baseline/cutoffs/valuation+coverage/provisional labels; authorised source drill-down | Live supported cost view part of costing slice; downloadable immutable artefact optional later |
| Managed work-pack / attachments | Job-controlled instructions/selected evidence with audience/version/retention binding | Later scoped adapter; no bulk export/archive of all files |

Mandatory first pilot: reliable authorised execution/outcome/closeout and supported cost views/records,
including their business versions and audit evidence. No mandatory formal rendered output by default.
If owner selects a real requirement for customer report/signature before fulfilment, its supported
evidence and0137 adapter become a path-specific gate; do not silently impose it on every service.
No provisional view or browser print may masquerade as archived official completion evidence.

## D1 — content ownership, purpose and access

Job owns document meaning: scope, baseline/plan/outcome, permitted audience, required fields, snapshot
event and retention/hold decisions. Purchasing/Inventory/labour provide only permitted owning-source
facts; summaries never recompute stock/value or transfer ownership. Company owns identity/brand facts.
Documents owns rendering/configuration/storage/generation/recovery mechanics. No new authoritative
fulfilment, approval, customer consent, stock movement or Finance status follows from generation.

Recommend explicit customer completion, internal operations and internal cost profiles with distinct
identities where formal output exists. No full financial original redacted in place and still called
original. Restricted reader receives permitted narrower profile or refusal; narrow summary says
authorised scope only, not complete Job total. File/config/template/recovery administration grants
no business read. Current actor/subject/company/audience rights apply to preview/history/download/
reprint/replay/cache/attachments; assignment or possession of a link grants none.

## D2 — timing and snapshot choices

Live work sheet reads current authorised effective facts; print/capture labels plan/source version,
as-of and draft/live status. Prefer live-first to reduce stale instructions. Offline execution,
sync, signed authority tokens and portal are deferred; paper never bypasses holds/termination.

Completion report references authoritative completed/partial/stopped event and exact evidence revision.
Recommend optional explicit generate/capture from that evidence; generation neither completes Job
nor proves customer acceptance. A customer receipt/signature, if later required, is separate governed
evidence with its own meaning/security, not a decorative signature line or download log.

Cost summary pins incurred cutoff, known/recorded-as-of, current-restated versus saved-dated basis,
selected estimate/baseline/plan, source-generation vector, allocation/correction chain and0147 statuses.
Label provisional versus supported valuation and known subtotal versus complete total. No arbitrary
historical report if source reconstruction unavailable. An optional saved dated output needs reliable
source snapshot/manifest, not rerunning current SQL for an old date.

Only when the business workflow requires a frozen document does owner transaction bind exact facts,
manifest and durable generation intent with0137 atomicity; render bytes later in bounded worker.
Optional after-the-fact report binds to its own immutable snapshot, not a fictitious completion-time
snapshot. Failed optional generation does not undo valid completion/closeout. A mandatory adapter
bind failure blocks only the affected operation whose contract requires it; rendering/storage DB
crash windows follow0137 and never claim a single DB/blob transaction.

## D3 — page/version handling and historical evidence

Use curated shared page profiles/first-continuation headers/footers/tables; A4 proposed initial Job
output, Letter only with adapter-specific verification. Long scope/activities/notes/evidence references
must paginate without clipping, duplicate totals or dropped rows. Repeat Job/report identity and
page/version/as-of context; customer profile never repeats an internal-cost footer.

Pin template/page/brand/assets/fonts/schema/renderer versions at formal snapshot binding. Current
preferences/brand edits cannot mutate official history. Reprint returns original bytes after current
access check; technical repair creates labelled linked rendition. Changed business outcome/cost basis
requires owning correction/new snapshot and successor output, preserving original and0151 linkage.
No new lifecycle status or numbering series assumed; use central numbering only where actual owner
policy requires an official document number. No counterfeit number on preview.

Attachments retain owner/audience association, safe validation/quarantine and immutable version where
relied on. No unsafe cross-subject attachment reuse or customer exposure of internal files. Retention
and legal holds reuse owner-approved policies, not a new universal duration or automatic purge rule.

## D4 — alternatives, delivery dependencies and proposed scope

**Live views only first (recommended default):** smallest operational pilot, no fictitious originals;
formal external sharing waits for genuine adapter requirement. **One optional customer report adapter:**
useful customer handover, but requires actual0137 infrastructure, approved projection/timing and storage/
recovery/security. **Mandatory PDF for every transition:** burdens SIMPLE and confuses execution with
generation; reject without a business requirement. **Independent Job renderer/archive:** duplicates0137;
reject. Existing convenience printing is not proof of shared formal artefact guarantees.

Future Job document delivery requires real Job/evidence/source snapshot contracts plus appropriate
DF registry/config/binding/render/archive/recovery/access, R9 guards/holder rollout and owner retention.
Required official outputs must not enable before secure original retrieval/recovery. Concurrent Job/
source correction races bind one exact snapshot or refuse stale generation. Fresh-key same generation
resolves original identity; changed intent conflicts; retry/worker rebuild never reposts economic effects.
Current limited readers cannot infer hidden costs from totals/pages/metadata/attachment counts/hashes.

Do not make later Finance, all module adapters or every customer document a universal service-pilot
dependency. Conversely, a chosen mandatory document requirement cannot be waived by an unimplemented
adapter or replaced with an unprotected browser file. Keep unsupported official outputs disabled.

## Genuine owner decisions requested

Review D1–D4: live-first default and no mandatory formal PDF; whether optional customer completion
report belongs in first pilot or later (recommend later unless customer need established); initial
A4/customer-safe/internal-cost profile boundaries and explicit snapshot generation timing. Recommend
no customer signature/consent functionality in first document scope. If required, identify actual
meaning and dependency before contract design; do not assume signature is universally required.

After direction, specify bounded Job document adapters/slices and consolidated pilot dependency plan.
Do not accept architecture or begin implementation from this bundle. Ordinary Job lifecycle remains
0145, closeout0151;0137 infrastructure guarantees are retained, not redesigned.
