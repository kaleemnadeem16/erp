# 0125 — Govern customer acceptance and explicit partial consent

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-14
- **Affects:** Phase 4 Sales customer decisions; R5/R7–R9 integration

## Context

Owner approval of the final C1–C8 review under planning Q46 settles customer acceptance architecture.
Issued quotation, customer agreement and operational targets must retain distinct immutable evidence.
This records business architecture only; it authorises no application implementation.

## Decision

C1/C2/C3/C6/C8 are accepted. FULL_ONLY is the default per independently acceptable logical scope.
Independent scopes and optional Items may be selected separately without authorising arbitrary
fractions. DIVISIBLE must be explicitly offered and supported by quantity bases, package/UoM
increments, minimums, technical scope, pricing entitlement, allocations and consent.

A smaller commitment has no inferred entitlement to the original bulk price. Changed terms use a
newly issued reoffer and fresh consent initially; no general acceptance-variation editor. A firm100
commitment delivered40/60 remains one100 commitment. Genuine earlier external agreement is preserved
and, where unsupported, referred to governed reconciliation, never invented consent to a new price.
An unchanged eligible firm offer is not repriced from today's masters. Declared continuing conditions
remain enforceable; hidden retrospective conditions cannot invalidate genuine customer agreement.

One accepted alternative binds each choose-one logical intent. First acceptance binds an exclusive
family claim; later compatible disjoint partials may consume its remaining capacity. Competitors are
claim-blocked, not automatically withdrawn/rejected. Independent nonexclusive scope stays independent.
Changing accepted choices/claims requires a separately governed correction.

Valid acceptance is separate from execution/handover/Finance readiness. Execution-only prerequisites
may remain pending; commercial identity, scope, terms, consent and required customer-facing facts
must be resolved. No universal CustomerAccount, Assembly or Job prerequisite is introduced.
Keep accepted commitments, available offers, claim-blocked scope, unoffered potential, withdrawn/
rejected remainder and fulfilment distinct. Preserve0101/0102 trust and explicit FX valuation:
partial acceptance neither wins the entire Opportunity nor counts revisions as new sales.

This refines downstream contracts under0100/0104/0109/0124 without changing their accepted bodies.
Partial-family replacement/reoffer, including later offers for unoffered remainder after an accepted
slice, remains refused until necessary R6 **and R7** contracts are approved **and delivered**.
Unsupported Assembly, fixed-charge, alternative and allocation paths remain disabled.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** real customer commitments remain attributable without invented terms or duplicate sales.
- **Negative:** supported acceptance requires explicit evidence and coordinated claims; ambiguous or
  unsupported cases stay pending/refused rather than being guessed into a successful transaction.
- **Requires:** the [domain contract](../domain/sales-customer-decisions.md), staged
  [delivery specification](../phases/phase-4-r6-implementation-slices.md), separately approved R7/R9
  dependencies and code authorisation before delivery. Documentation is not implementation verification.

## Alternatives considered

Always retain bulk prices or silently reprice a smaller purchase — either invents entitlement or
changes consent. A general variation editor initially — adds an unnecessary sensitive terms writer.
Automatic Job creation/competitor cancellation — confuses distinct business facts and loses evidence.
