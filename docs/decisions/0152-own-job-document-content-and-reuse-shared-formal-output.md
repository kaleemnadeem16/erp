# 0152 — Own Job document content and reuse shared formal output

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-16
- **Affects:** Phase5 D1–D4/Q57; Job views/evidence and later0137 adapters
- **Authority:** owner approval; architecture and readiness planning only, not application delivery

## Context

Service execution needs current instructions and durable outcomes, without a PDF at every transition.
0137 already owns formal output; another Job renderer/archive or financial payload hidden visually in
customer reports would violate its boundaries.

## Decision

Job owns scope/instructions/outcomes, audiences and business snapshots; source owners provide authorised
facts; Company supplies supported identity/branding; Documents0137 owns formal configuration/rendering/
private storage/recovery/reprints. Separate customer-safe, internal operations and internal cost
profiles; never render full internal data and merely hide confidential sections. Generation changes
no fulfilment, consent, stock, financial obligation or execution authority.

Live-first work sheets show current authorised scope, assignment, instructions, plan version and
effective restrictions. Printed/saved sheets state version/as-of and cannot authorise work after a
hold/cancellation. Pilot requires live work/operational/cost views and durable completion/closeout
business evidence, not PDFs for ordinary transitions. Optional generation failure leaves valid
completion intact. Formal customer reports, immutable downloadable cost artefacts, signatures/consent
and advanced work packs/archives are optional and deferred.

Formal completion reports bind supported authoritative outcomes, not create completion/consent. Freeze
documents only for genuine requirements using0137 durable intent/versioning. Prefer A4; verify other
formats individually. Preserve exact original evidence/template/brand/version/audience and correct
long-content pagination. Reprint original bytes under current access. Changed business meaning needs
owning correction/linked successor; technical repairs follow0137 labelled rendition checks. Attachments
remain private and subject/audience-scoped.

Documents is not a universal blocker to a no-formal-output pilot. A path explicitly requiring protected
formal output waits for real0137 infrastructure and Job adapter; browser printing/type registration
is insufficient. Preserve0137 and quotation-first DF0–DF5 without redesign or duplication.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** useful SIMPLE execution without PDF ceremony or confidential-data leakage.
- **Negative:** formal sharing waits for real adapter/security/storage/recovery delivery.
- **Requires:** [bounded contract](../domain/job-operational-documents.md), current-authorised views,
  durable evidence and path-specific output gates. No application/grants/migrations authorised.

## Alternatives considered

Mandatory PDF per transition — confuses business evidence and rendering without universal need.
Independent renderer/archive — duplicates0137. Visually hidden internal payload — leaks facts.
Browser print as archived original — lacks actual framework guarantees.

## Research basis

Reuse [Q57 evidence/options/scenarios](../phases/phase-5-operational-documents-research.md) and0137's
Frappe/Business Central content/layout comparison (accessed2026-10-15). Owner selects live-first/no
formal pilot output, preserving existing guarantees; no repeated vendor search required.
